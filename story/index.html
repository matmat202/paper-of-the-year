Coda · The papers themselves

Every year has a paper

The site this story was cut from names Stanford's most-cited work for every year since 1900, filterable by the same four domains: Paper of the Year.

Counts are OpenAlex works of type article, review, book or book chapter, not retracted, with an author whose institution lineage includes Stanford (I97018004, which also counts Stanford Medicine and Stanford Health Care); the world is the same filter without the Stanford condition. A work's domain is that of its primary topic, which OpenAlex assigns by classifier; works with no topic (at most 1.1% of any period) are left out of both. Hospital affiliations may be credited to Stanford more completely in recent decades than in old records, which would lift the late health share; the world's own share is the check that the turn is not simply science's.
