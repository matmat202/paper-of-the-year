Paper of the Year

Stanford's most-cited paper for every year since 1900, by citations in OpenAlex. Stanford authors are in bold. Older papers have had longer to be cited, so compare a year with its neighbours, not with 2024.

      Data: OpenAlex (CC0), works with an author affiliated with Stanford or Stanford Medicine, articles, reviews, books and chapters only, retractions excluded. Citation counts are OpenAlex's and differ from Google Scholar's. A reprint is dated by its reprint, so a title already listed in an earlier year is dropped from later ones, book reviews and corrections are skipped, and a few re-dated classics are excluded by hand ().
